## Why Remington has 48,228 ratings but only 319 units a month

**This is what a legacy listing looks like, and the pattern is worth unpacking because it is easy to misread.**

| Metric | Ceramic Ionic | Slopehill 1902 | Wavytalk 1875W |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ratings count** | **48,228** | 22,195 | 32,950 |
| **Est. units/month** | **319** | **8,660** | 2,948 |
| **Category BSR** | 9 | **1** | 3 |
| **Days on sale** | 1,959 (~5.4 yr) | 1,153 (~3.2 yr) | 1,716 (~4.7 yr) |

**Reviews accumulate; sales velocity does not.** A product on sale for 5.4 years collects ratings the whole time, so a long-lived listing carries a large cumulative total even when its current monthly volume is modest. The slopehill, by contrast, has been on sale for 3.2 years, has half the ratings, and outsells the Remington by roughly **27×**.

**What that means practically.** A huge ratings count tells you the product has been around a long time and that most buyers were satisfied. It does **not** tell you the product is currently in demand, currently well-stocked, or currently the best option at its price. Those are different questions.

**The second signal in the same data.** Our keyword research shows only about **ten searches a month** in Canada for Remington hair dryer terms. Yet the products sell steadily. **That combination points to shelf-driven purchasing** — people buying in Walmart Canada, Canadian Tire or similar, not researching online first.

**Why that matters to you.** If you are reading this, you are in the minority who researches. The 4.4–4.5★ ratings are real and the sample is large, but you have access to information the typical Remington buyer did not, and it is worth using — see the comparison further down.

## Remington hair dryer FAQ

**Is Remington a good hair dryer brand?**

**Why does the Remington Ceramic Ionic have 48,228 ratings but low sales?**
It is what a legacy listing looks like. Reviews accumulate over years, so a long-lived product carries a large total even when current velocity is modest. At roughly 319 units a month against 48,228 ratings, it has been on sale for about 5.4 years. High total ratings do not indicate high current demand.

**Where do people usually buy Remington hair dryers?**
Mostly in physical retail rather than through search. Our keyword data shows only about ten searches a month for Remington hair dryer terms in Canada, while the products sell steadily in our sales data. That pattern — high sales, negligible search volume — points to shelf-driven purchasing in stores such as Walmart Canada and Canadian Tire.

Neither of the two Canadian models we track is marketed as dual voltage, so treat them as home dryers. Voltage was the single most-discussed topic in our entire dataset at 785 mentions. For travel, see our [travel hair dryer guide](/hair-types/travel-hair-dryer/).

**Is Remington good for fine hair?**
Partly. Both models sit in the budget band where our data shows a 30.3% complaint rate, which is the main caveat. On the plus side, the Damage Protection model is explicitly positioned around reduced heat damage, which is the right concern for fine hair — see our [fine hair guide](/hair-types/fine-hair/).

**How long does a Remington hair dryer last?**
We cannot say specifically. Across 8,261 Canadian reviews the median reported failure was 5.5 months, and dryers under C$50 had a 30.3% complaint rate against 9.6% over C$120. Both Remington models sit in the under-C$50 band.
