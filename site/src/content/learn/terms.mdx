## Accuracy, prices and specifications

**We work to be accurate, and we are explicit about the limits.**

**Prices change frequently.** Every price on this site carries the date we verified it, and we re-check commercial pages monthly. **But the price that applies is the one shown on the retailer's page when you buy** — not the one shown here. Always confirm before purchasing.

**Specifications come from different sources, and we label them.** Where a specification comes from a manufacturer rather than from our own measurement, it is labelled as a manufacturer claim. We have not bench-tested every model we cover, and our guides say so rather than presenting estimates as measurements.

**Our review analysis has stated limitations.** The review dataset we use skews toward recent and critical reviews, which is why its average rating differs from the ratings displayed on retailer pages. We publish that caveat on our [methodology page](/how-we-test/) and use the data for patterns rather than as a substitute for a product's displayed rating.

**Availability changes.** A product recommended in a guide may be discontinued, out of stock, or sold by a different seller by the time you read it. Seller structure in particular can change, and seller structure affects who honours a warranty.

**We do not warrant that any content is complete, current or error-free.** If you find an error, tell us — see [contact](/contact/) — and we will correct it and change the page's last-updated date.

## No professional advice

**Nothing on this site is medical, dermatological, electrical, safety or professional advice.**

Hair dryers are electrical appliances that generate heat, and our guides describe reported buyer experiences and publicly available data. They do not replace the manufacturer's instructions, the safety information supplied with a product, or the advice of a qualified professional.

If you have a scalp or skin condition, an allergy, or a concern about using an electrical appliance, consult a qualified professional. If you are unsure whether an appliance is safe to use, stop using it and consult the manufacturer or a qualified electrician.

**Safety checks we describe, such as inspecting an ALCI plug, are general guidance.** They are not a certification that any particular unit is safe, and they do not replace the product's own safety documentation.
