## How we structure a comparison

**Every comparison follows the same order, so you can jump to what matters to you.**

1. **Quick answer** — who each product suits, before any detail

4. **Drying performance** — the airflow approach, and where we have not measured

10. **Travel and storage** — where voltage decisions live
11. **Durability and warranty** — where complaint rates by price band appear
12. **Value over time** — cost per year, with the assumptions stated
13. **Who should buy each one** — including who should not
14. **Alternatives** — including cheaper and more expensive options
15. **FAQ**

## Two rules we apply to every comparison

**1. We do not declare a winner.** A dryer that is better for travel is usually not the one that is better for thick hair. Ranking one above the other overall would hide the fact that matters. We tell you which buyer each product suits.

**2. We state when a comparison is uneven.** A high-velocity hair dryer and a multi-styler are different product categories. A C$100 dryer and a C$630 dryer are not equivalents. Where either is true, we say so at the top rather than letting the table imply a like-for-like contest.

**What is not a comparison input:** commission rate. Our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) sets out why, and how it is enforced.

## Frequently asked questions

**What makes a fair hair dryer comparison?**
Comparing products that do the same job. A high-velocity hair dryer and a multi-styler are different categories, and comparing them produces a meaningless result. Where product types differ we say so rather than forcing a comparison.

**Why do your comparisons not declare a winner?**
Because a winner depends on what you value. A dryer that is better for travel is usually not the one that is better for thick hair. We give you the difference and tell you which buyer each product suits, rather than declaring an overall champion.

**Do you compare across price ranges?**
Yes, and we say when the comparison is uneven. Laifen vs Dyson is a comparison between a C$100 dryer and a C$400–630 dryer, and we state that plainly instead of presenting them as equivalents.

**How do you handle products you have not tested?**
We compare what we can verify: price, rating, seller structure, review-evidence patterns and manufacturer claims labelled as such. Where we have not measured airflow, weight or noise, the comparison table says not yet tested rather than carrying an estimate.

**Which hair dryer comparisons should I read first?**
If you are weighing a premium import against a cheaper alternative, start with Laifen vs Dyson. If you want the mid-price option that adds scalp protection, read Shark vs Dyson.
