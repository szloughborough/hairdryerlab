## Wavytalk 1875W performance and heat

**What we can say.** The Wavytalk is a conventional 1875W dryer, which means the heating element does more of the work than the motor. High-speed brushless dryers invert that — they move a large volume of air at lower temperature *(manufacturer claim)*.

**Why the distinction matters for your hair type.** Drying is fundamentally a water-removal problem, and moving air removes water faster than raising its temperature does. That is why airflow dominates our complaint data and why reviewers with thick hair describe conventional dryers as inadequate rather than as too hot.

**For which hair types the Wavytalk works.** Fine to medium hair that is not heat-sensitive, and shorter hair where the total drying time is small. It includes a diffuser, so it is usable on wavy hair if the diffuser holds.

**For which it does not.** Thick, coarse or long hair, where you want airflow. And heat-sensitive, damaged or colour-treated hair, where a heat-first design works against you. Our [thick hair guide](/hair-types/thick-hair/) and [fine hair guide](/hair-types/fine-hair/) cover what to look for instead.

**What we cannot say.** How hot it actually runs, how fast it dries, or how it compares on airflow to any specific competitor. We have not measured any of it, and we do not publish estimates.
