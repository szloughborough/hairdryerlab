## What we liked about the Dreame Pocket Pro

**1. It is the joint-highest-rated dryer above C$120 in our dataset at 4.6★.**
That is measured against 334 reviews in the over-C$120 band. Only the Laifen Air Diffuser matches it, and it does so at a lower price *(Amazon.ca data)*.

**2. Dual voltage is genuinely rare here.**
Voltage was the single most-discussed topic in our entire dataset at **785 mentions** — ahead of noise, weight and price. Most Canadian dryers are 120V only, so a high-speed dryer that works abroad is a real functional advantage rather than a specification nicety *(review analysis)*.

**3. A diffuser that is matched to the dryer.**
Attachment security generated **186 mentions in our dataset with 57% negative**, and the recurring complaint is parts falling off. A diffuser that ships with the dryer is sized to its barrel and holds better than a universal aftermarket part *(review analysis)*.

**4. Brand-operated listing with a single seller.**
The Buy Box is held by **Dreame Technology HK** with no competing sellers. Warranty and service complaints were the second-largest cluster in our data at **610 negative mentions and an 81% negative share**, and a large part of that comes from reseller listings where nobody owns the claim. This listing avoids that *(Amazon.ca data)*.

**5. It sits in the price band where complaints fall sharply.**
Over C$120, the complaint rate in our data is **9.6%** across 334 reviews, against **30.3%** under C$50 across 3,259 *(review analysis)*.

## Dreame Pocket Pro drying performance

**What we can say:** the Dreame Pocket Pro is a high-speed brushless dryer, which means it moves a large volume of air at a lower temperature rather than relying on a hot element. That is the same approach Laifen and Dyson use, and it is the right one for dense or long hair *(manufacturer claim)*.

**Why the approach matters.** Airflow was the most-discussed topic in our dataset at **1,585 mentions**, and the complaint pattern runs one way: reviewers rarely complain a dryer is too powerful, they complain it is too weak for their hair. Underpowered dryers are the most common single reason reviewers describe returning one *(review analysis)*.

**What we cannot say:** how fast it actually dries. We have not measured airflow velocity or dry time on a standardised swatch. When the unit completes our protocol, those numbers get published on the Lab Test Card and this review gets a new last-updated date.

**Practical guidance:** if your hair is thick or long and drying speed is your priority, the Laifen SE2 at about C$145 is specifically tuned for that buyer and costs C$16 less. The Dreame's case rests on travel, not on power.

## Dreame Pocket Pro heat and settings

**Heat control is the second-largest complaint cluster in our dataset, so it is worth examining on any dryer.**

- The Pocket Pro uses a high-speed brushless motor, which generates less heat for the same airflow than a conventional element *(manufacturer claim)*
- Dyson's Supersonic Nural takes heat control further with active heat sensing, which the Dreame does not claim *(manufacturer claim)*
- **Temperature and overheating produced 590 negative mentions in our data, 54% of all heat-related comments** *(review analysis)*

**The failure pattern in our data is hardware, not control logic.** The severe outcomes we coded — smoke at 182 mentions with 96% negative, melting plastic at 64 with 89% negative — are component failures. Better heat control does not protect you from a cord that fails at the handle junction.

**Two safety checks worth running on arrival regardless of the review you are reading:** the **ALCI plug** should have a rectangular GFCI-style box, and the **cord strain relief** where it enters the handle should not flex easily. Cord and connector failure accounted for **417 mentions** in our dataset, second only to complete stoppage at 601 — see [how long hair dryers actually last](/guides/how-long-does-a-hair-dryer-last/).

## Dreame Pocket Pro attachments and diffuser

**This is the second concrete advantage over the Laifen Air, and it is not cosmetic.**

- **The diffuser ships with the dryer** rather than as a separate variant, so the fit is matched to the barrel *(manufacturer claim)*
- Our data shows **186 mentions of attachment problems with 57% negative**, and "the nozzle keeps falling off" is the recurring phrasing *(review analysis)*
- One reviewer described a nozzle that had **melted** where it sat against the barrel *(buyer feedback)*
- Universal aftermarket diffusers fit no barrel precisely, which is the most likely route to the falling-off problem

**The test to run on arrival:** fit the diffuser, invert the dryer, and shake it gently ten times. Do that on day one, inside the return window. It takes thirty seconds and it is the single most common curly-hair complaint we found.

## Dreame Pocket Pro dual voltage and travel

**This is the reason to buy this dryer, so it deserves its own section.**

| Situation | What the Pocket Pro offers |
|---|---|
| Using it in Canada | Standard 120V operation |
| Europe, UK, Australia | **Dual voltage**, with a plug adapter *(manufacturer claim)* |
| United States | No change needed |
| Hotel-supplied dryers | Lightweight and usually weak — carrying your own avoids this |
| Curly hair abroad | Diffuser included, which hotel dryers never have |

**What a plug adapter cannot do.** An adapter changes the shape of the plug, not the voltage. A single-voltage Canadian dryer plugged into a European 220–240V socket is damaged, sometimes immediately. A converter capable of running a hair dryer at 1,600W+ costs more than this dryer does.

**The honest caveat:** confirm the exact voltage range on the current listing before you travel. Models get revised, and we are working from the listing data we hold at a stated date. Our [travel hair dryer guide](/hair-types/travel-hair-dryer/) covers the alternatives, including the Dyson Supersonic Travel at about C$400 and the Revlon RVDR5034F at about C$19.

## Dreame Pocket Pro FAQ

**Is the Dreame Pocket Pro worth it?**
Yes, if dual voltage matters to you. It is the joint-highest-rated dryer above C$120 in our Canadian dataset at 4.6 stars, it includes a diffuser, and it is sold through a brand-operated listing with a single seller. If you never travel, the Laifen Air at about C$100 delivers a similar high-speed approach for C$60 less.

**Is the Dreame Pocket Pro dual voltage?**
Yes, and that is its main advantage over almost every other high-speed dryer sold in Canada. Voltage was the single most-discussed topic in our entire dataset at 785 mentions, and most Canadian dryers are 120V only. Confirm the exact range on the listing before you travel, since models are revised.

**How does the Dreame Pocket Pro compare to the Laifen Air?**
The Dreame costs C$60 more at about C$160 against about C$100, and rates 0.1 stars higher at 4.6 against 4.5. What you get for the difference is dual voltage and a diffuser included as standard. If neither matters to you, the Laifen Air is the better value.

**Does the Dreame Pocket Pro come with a diffuser?**
Yes. That matters more than it sounds, because attachment security is the most common curly-hair complaint in our data at 186 mentions and 57% negative. A diffuser that ships with the dryer is matched to its barrel diameter, which holds better than a universal aftermarket part.

**Why does the Dreame Pocket Pro only sell about 163 units a month?**
Its price is the main reason. At about C$160 it sits above the Laifen Air at about C$100 and competes with entry Dyson pricing. Low Canadian volume is the honest limitation on this review: there are not yet enough long-term Canadian owners to judge reliability.

**Is the Dreame Pocket Pro good for thick hair?**
It is a high-speed brushless dryer, so it moves air rather than relying on heat, which is the right approach for dense hair. But at about C$160 the Laifen SE2 is specifically tuned for thick or long hair and costs C$16 less, so compare those two before deciding.

## Our verdict on the Dreame Pocket Pro

**Recommended — for travellers, and for anyone who wants a diffuser included.**

The Dreame Pocket Pro earns a recommendation on three things we can verify: **4.6★ from 252 Canadian ratings**, which is the joint-highest above C$120 in our dataset; **dual voltage**, which almost no high-speed dryer sold in Canada offers; and a **brand-operated single-seller listing**, which gives you one counterparty if it fails.

**What holds it back is evidence, not performance.** At 163 units a month there are not enough Canadian owners to say how it behaves in year three, and we have not bench-tested it. We would rather state that than imply a confidence we do not have.

**Buy it if you travel.** If you do not, the **Laifen Air at about C$100** is the better value and the **Laifen SE2 at about C$145** is the better pick for dense hair.
