## How our reviews are structured

**Every review answers the same set of questions, so you can compare like for like.**

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Best for / Skip if** | Who should buy it, and who should not — before you read further |
| **Price and rating** | Verified on Amazon.ca, with the check date stated |
| **What we liked** | The strengths the evidence actually supports |
| **What we didn't** | The weaknesses, including for our top picks |
| **Reliability signals** | Price band complaint rate, seller structure and Buy Box holder |
| **Comparison table** | Competing models side by side, with measured rows marked where we have not tested yet |
| **FAQ** | The questions buyers actually ask |

**We do not publish a score out of ten.** A single number hides the trade-offs, and in a category where a C$43 dryer and a C$630 dryer are both "good", the trade-offs are the whole decision.

## What we check for every review

**Four things, and all of them are decisions you can verify yourself.**

1. **Reliability signals from our review analysis.** Durability is the most negative topic in our dataset at 946 negative mentions and an 84% negative share, so complaint rate by price band is a ranking input rather than a footnote
2. **Seller structure.** Whether the listing is brand-operated and who holds the Buy Box, because that determines whether a warranty claim has a counterparty
3. **Verified price and rating** at a stated check date
4. **Fit for a specific buyer** — most people, thick hair, curly hair, budget or premium

**What is not an input:** commission rate, brand relationship or advertising spend. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do you decide which hair dryers to review?**
We start from the Amazon.ca Hair Dryers category ranking, which shows what is actually selling in Canada, rather than from what brands pitch us. Models need enough Canadian sales or reviews that a reader might genuinely be considering them, and we prioritise products where our data reveals something a buyer would not otherwise know.

**Why do some reviews cover several models at once?**
Where a brand sells several similar models with confusingly similar names, a single brand-level review is more useful than separate pages that repeat each other. Our Laifen review covers all six Canadian models in one place precisely because the names overlap badly and buyers order the wrong one.

**Do your reviews include a score out of ten?**
No. We give category conclusions such as drying speed, noise, weight, heat control and value, and we show the underlying figures where we have them. A single composite number hides the trade-offs that actually decide a purchase.

**How current are your reviews?**
Prices and ratings are re-checked monthly, and each comparison table states the date the figures were verified. Where a specification cannot be verified for the exact variant, we say so rather than printing a number we cannot stand behind.

**Will you review more models?**
Yes. We are expanding coverage by brand and by hair type. If a model you are considering is missing, tell us at editorial@hairdryerlab.ca — reader requests are one of the inputs we use when deciding what to cover next.
