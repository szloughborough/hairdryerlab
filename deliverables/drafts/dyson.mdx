## Is the Dyson hair dryer worth it?

**This is the question every Dyson page dodges, so here is our actual position.**

**What the C$300 to C$530 premium over a Laifen buys you:**

| You get | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **A Canadian service network** | No other brand in this comparison has one. When a dryer fails at month seven, this is the difference between a process and a dead end |
| **A decade of product history** | The Supersonic family predates every competitor here. Longevity is not a specification, but it is evidence |
| **Universal voltage on the Travel** | A genuine functional difference, not a marketing one |
| **Heat sensing on the Nural** | The most sophisticated heat control available, relevant if your hair is fine or damaged |
| **A brand-operated listing** | One seller, one warranty counterparty — though Laifen, Dreame, wavytalk and Aina also do this |

**What it does not buy you:**

- **A higher rating.** Both Dyson models rate below a Laifen Air Diffuser and a Dreame Pocket Pro *(Amazon.ca data)*
- **More airflow than the data supports.** Dyson describes the same high-airflow, lower-heat approach every premium competitor describes *(manufacturer claim)*
- **Reliability evidence specific to these two SKUs.** The Travel has been on sale 6.5 months and the Nural 12.4 — anyone claiming long-term reliability data for either is guessing *(Amazon.ca data)*

**Our honest read.** If a failure at month seven would be a serious problem for you, or if you travel internationally, the premium buys something concrete and the answer is yes. If you are comparing purely on drying experience per dollar, the answer is no — and our data explains why: **the over-C$120 price band has a 9.6% complaint rate, and every model on this page sits inside that band** *(review analysis)*. The band is what predicts reliability, and Laifen and Dreame are in it too.

## Dyson hair dryer FAQ

**Is the Dyson hair dryer worth the price?**
Only if you value what the premium actually buys: a Canadian service network, a decade of product history, and universal voltage on the Travel model. On rating alone it does not win — both Dyson models rate below a Laifen and a Dreame that cost a quarter as much.

**Why does Dyson rate lower than cheaper hair dryers?**
We cannot fully explain it, and we will not guess. What we can report is the data: Supersonic Travel is 4.4 stars from 310 Canadian ratings and the Nural is 4.5 from 125, while the Laifen Air Diffuser and Dreame Pocket Pro both sit at 4.6. Price and satisfaction are not the same axis.

**What is the difference between Dyson Supersonic Travel and Nural?**
The Travel at about C$400 is the lighter, more compact variant with universal voltage for international use. The Nural at about C$630 adds heat sensing that adjusts to distance from the head, which is the more conservative option for fine or damaged hair. The Nural is C$230 more.

**Does the Dyson Supersonic work in Europe?**
The Supersonic Travel does, using universal voltage, so you need only a plug adapter. Confirm the exact range on the listing before you travel, because models are revised. Voltage was the single most-discussed topic in our entire dataset at 785 mentions.

**Is Dyson sold by the brand in Canada?**
Yes. Both Dyson models we track have the Buy Box held by Dyson itself, with a single seller and no competing resellers. That is the cleanest warranty structure in our dataset alongside Laifen, Dreame, wavytalk and Aina.

**How much of the Canadian market does Dyson have?**
About 1.2% of unit sales in the Amazon.ca top 50, but around 11.5% of revenue. Dyson sells few dryers at a very high price, which is a completely different position from slopehill, which holds about 30% of units at about C$43.

## Our verdict on Dyson hair dryers

**Buy the Dyson Supersonic Travel at about C$400 if you travel internationally or want a Canadian service network behind the warranty.** It is the better value of the two Dyson models, has the larger review base at 310 ratings, and universal voltage is a real functional advantage rather than a marketing one.

**Buy the Dyson Supersonic Nural at about C$630 only if heat sensing is worth C$230 to you.** It is the most advanced heat control in the category and the right choice for fine, damaged or colour-treated hair — but at 6.3× a Laifen Air, and with the smallest review sample of any premium model we cover, it is a considered purchase rather than an obvious one.

**Do not buy Dyson expecting the highest rating.** It does not have it. The Laifen Air Diffuser and Dreame Pocket Pro both sit at **4.6★** against Dyson's 4.4★ and 4.5★. What Dyson has is the service network, the track record and the travel voltage — and those are the reasons to pay, if you pay.
