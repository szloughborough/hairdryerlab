# About Hair Dryer Lab

**Hair Dryer Lab is an independent hair dryer research site for Canadian buyers.** We are not a retailer and we are not owned by a hair dryer brand. Our guides are built from a coded analysis of thousands of verified Canadian buyer reviews, combined with current Amazon.ca listing data. We have not completed bench testing yet — and we say so on every guide rather than filling the gap with estimates.

---

## What Hair Dryer Lab is

**We are a research and buying-guidance publication, not a shop.** We do not sell hair dryers, we do not hold stock, and we do not process orders. When you decide to buy, you buy from a retailer such as Amazon.ca, and our job ends at helping you decide which model to buy.

**What that means in practice:**
- Every commercial page tells you **what we checked, when we checked it, and what we did not check**
- Every recommendation includes **what we did not like** about it, not just what worked
- Prices carry the **date we verified them**, because a price without a date is not information
- We name the **seller and the Buy Box holder** for each product, because that determines who honours your warranty

**Where we are focused.** Canadian buyers only. That means Canadian dollars, Canadian retailers, Canadian warranty handling, the ALCI plug standard, dual-voltage questions for travel, and the practical differences — duty, brokerage, bilingual documentation — that separate buying in Canada from buying in the United States.

---

## Why we started

**Buying a hair dryer in Canada is harder than it should be.** The category has fragmented into high-speed brushless dryers, legacy budget brands, and premium imports, and almost all the guidance available is either rewritten from manufacturer bullet points or written for a US audience by people who have never checked a Canadian price.

The specific problem we set out to solve was this: **the questions buyers actually ask are not the questions the category answers.** The listings talk about motors and ion counts. Buyers ask whether it will still work next year.

So we started with the evidence that already exists in public — thousands of reviews written by Canadians who actually lived with these products — and coded it systematically instead of reading a handful and generalising.

---

## How we research hair dryers

**Our method has three layers, and we label which layer every claim comes from.**

**1. Review analysis — our primary evidence.** We analyse verified Amazon.ca reviews at scale, coding each one against 20 concern categories and tagging it with its star rating. That lets us separate what people talk about from where they are unhappy. When we report that the median reported failure time is **5.5 months**, it comes from 552 reviews that named a specific month, not from a handful of complaints. Our full method is published on [how we test](/how-we-test/).

**2. Listing data.** For every recommended model we record the CAD price, the Amazon.ca rating and rating count, estimated monthly unit sales, how long the listing has been live, how many sellers compete, and who holds the Buy Box. All of it is verifiable by you the same day.

**3. Bench testing — in progress.** We have not completed hands-on measurement of the models we recommend, and we do not pretend otherwise. Our protocol covers airflow velocity, sound pressure at 30 cm and 1 metre, weight with cord, dry time on a standardised swatch, surface temperature, attachment security and ALCI plug inspection. When each unit completes the protocol we publish the numbers and date the change.

## What we have not done yet

**We think this section belongs on an About page, and almost no review site has one.**

| Workstream | Status | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Review analysis** | Complete and ongoing | Thousands of verified Canadian reviews coded across 20 concern categories |
| **Listing data capture** | Complete, re-checked monthly | Price, rating, seller count and Buy Box verified at a stated date |
| **Bench testing** | **Not started** | No measured airflow, noise, weight or dry time published yet |
| **Long-term durability tracking** | **Not started** | Our 12-month tracking begins with the current test units |
| **Own product photography** | **Not started** | Product images currently come from retailer listings |
| **Canadian model coverage** | Partial | We cover the models Canadians actually buy, not everything on sale |

**We would rather publish a shorter guide with honest gaps than a longer one padded with numbers we did not measure.**

## Our editorial principles

**These are the rules that decide what we publish, and they are not aspirational — they have already changed our recommendations.**

**1. We rank on what breaks.** "Durability / it broke" is the most negative topic in our review dataset, with 946 negative mentions and an 84% negative share. That is why our comparison tables lead with warranty, expected life and complaint rate by price band rather than peak airflow.

**2. Every recommendation carries its weaknesses.** A guide with no criticism is not a guide. Our top picks all have a "what we didn't like" section, and we name models to avoid when the evidence supports it.

**3. We do not invent measurements.** No number appears on this site that we did not measure or source. Where a specification comes from a brand, it is labelled as a manufacturer claim. Where it comes from our review analysis, it is labelled as such.

**4. We state the limits of our own data.** Our review sample averages 3.37 stars while the same products display 4.3–4.6 stars on Amazon, because the export skews toward recent and critical reviews. We publish that caveat rather than hiding it, and we use the data for patterns only — never as a substitute for a product's displayed rating.

**5. Trust beats promotion.** When there is a conflict between making a page look more promotional and making it more trustworthy, we choose trust. When there is a conflict between adding more design and making the information clearer, we choose clarity.

**6. The accurate recommendation beats the profitable one.** When there is a conflict between maximising affiliate clicks and giving the buyer a more accurate recommendation, we give the accurate recommendation. This is written into our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## How we make money

**Some links on this site are affiliate links.** If you buy a product through one, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. That commission is how the site is funded.

**Two things follow from that, and both are commitments rather than claims:**
- **Commission rates do not set our rankings.** Our recommendations have favoured products on the strength of durability data, price band and seller structure.
- **Brands cannot pay to change a conclusion.** No brand has paid for a ranking, a rating, or the removal of a criticism.

We describe the detail in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/), and the rules governing editorial independence are in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who runs Hair Dryer Lab?**
Hair Dryer Lab is produced by an editorial team, not a single named personality. We write under a team byline because that is what we actually are, rather than inventing an expert identity. Our research method and editorial rules are both published.

**Is Hair Dryer Lab independent?**
Yes. We accept affiliate commission from retailers, which is disclosed on every commercial page, but commission rates do not set our rankings. Our rankings follow the review analysis and listing data described on our methodology page.

**Where is Hair Dryer Lab based?**
Our research is focused entirely on the Canadian market. We work in Canadian dollars, check Canadian retailers and pricing, and cover Canadian-specific issues such as ALCI plug compliance and warranty handling.

**Do you accept payment from hair dryer brands?**
No brand has paid for a ranking, a rating, or the removal of a criticism. If we ever publish sponsored content it will be labelled as such and kept out of rankings. Our current commercial relationships are affiliate links only.

**How can I contact Hair Dryer Lab?**
Email editorial@hairdryerlab.ca. We take corrections seriously and will update a page and change its last-updated date when we get something wrong, rather than quietly editing it.
