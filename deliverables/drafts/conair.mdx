# Conair Hair Dryer Review

**The honest starting point: Conair is not a high-speed brushless dryer.** All three Canadian models we track are conventional 1875W designs that rely more on heat than on moving air. If drying speed or airflow is your priority, a Laifen, Dreame or Dyson will beat them.

**What Conair does offer is real, though.** The 318RC carries **9,491 ratings at 4.4★** in Canada, Conair has supported Canadian warranties for decades, and the brand holds roughly **17.9% of unit sales** in the Canadian category — second only to slopehill. If you want the lowest-risk cheap dryer and you do not have thick or long hair, that combination is genuinely hard to beat.

## Is Conair a reliable brand?

**Conair has the longest service history in this category, and its models still sit in the price band where complaints are most common. Both things are true.**

**What the brand brings:**

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Canadian warranty support** | **Decades of history** — the strongest of any brand we cover |
| **Review base** | 9,491 ratings on the 318RC alone, at 4.4★ |

| **Motor technology** | Conventional 1875W, not high-speed brushless |
| **Warranty counterparty** | Amazon or a third party — **no brand-operated listing** |

**What the category data says about the price band:**

| Price band | Reviews | Average rating | Complaint rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Under C$50** | 3,259 | 3.39★ | **30.3%** |
| C$50–120 | 887 | 3.52★ | 29.0% |
| Over C$120 | 334 | **4.43★** | **9.6%** |

**All three Conair models sit in the budget band.** The 30.3% figure is not a criticism of Conair specifically — it describes the band, which every budget brand competes in. But it does mean a Conair carries roughly three times the complaint rate of a dryer over C$120 *(review analysis)*.

**The one structural gap.** Unlike Laifen, Dreame and wavytalk — all of which operate brand-controlled Amazon.ca listings — **Conair has no brand-operated storefront** among the models we track. All three have Amazon or a third party holding the Buy Box with two or three competing sellers. Warranty and service complaints were the second-largest cluster in our dataset at **610 negative mentions and an 81% negative share**, and reseller listings where nobody owns the claim are a large contributor *(review analysis)*.

**Where that leaves the recommendation.** Conair is a good low-risk budget choice because of the service history and the enormous satisfied buyer base. It is not a performance choice, and the honest way to say it is that you are trading drying speed and airflow for price and support.

## Conair hair dryer FAQ

**Is Conair a good hair dryer brand?**
It depends what you want. Conair is not a high-speed brushless dryer, so if drying speed is your priority a Laifen or Dyson will beat it. What Conair offers is decades of Canadian warranty support and a very large satisfied buyer base — 9,491 ratings at 4.4 stars on the 318RC alone.

**Which Conair hair dryer is best?**
The InfinitiPro FloMotion Pro at about C$61 is the most capable, with 1,079 ratings at 4.5 stars and around 1,081 units a month. The 318RC at about C$25 is the best value and the highest-volume Conair in Canada at roughly 1,957 units a month.

**Does Conair make a high-speed brushless hair dryer?**
Not in the Canadian models we track. All three Conair listings in our dataset are conventional 1875W designs that rely more on heat than on airflow. That is a real limitation if your hair is thick or long, where airflow is what actually shortens drying time.

**Is Conair sold by the brand on Amazon.ca?**
No. All three Conair listings in our dataset have Amazon or a third party holding the Buy Box, with two or three competing sellers. Unlike Laifen, Dreame and wavytalk, there is no brand-operated storefront here — so the warranty counterparty is less clear.

**Why does Conair have such a low complaint rate in your data?**
It does not, and we want to be precise. Conair's models sit in the under-C$50 and C$50–60 bands, where our data shows a 30.3% complaint rate across 3,259 reviews, against 9.6% for dryers over C$120. The high star ratings reflect that most buyers are satisfied, not that complaints are rare.

**Is Conair a good choice for fine hair?**
Partly. It is a heat-first design, which is the opposite of what fine hair wants. But it does offer multiple heat settings and a cool shot at a very low price, and Conair has a genuine Canadian service history. See our [fine hair guide](/hair-types/fine-hair/) for the trade-offs.

## Our verdict on Conair hair dryers

**Conair is a good low-risk budget choice and a poor performance choice. Both halves of that sentence matter.**

**Buy the 318RC at about C$25** if you want the lowest-risk cheap dryer in Canada. **9,491 ratings at 4.4★**, 6.5 years on sale, roughly 1,957 units a month, and a brand that has supported Canadian warranties for decades. For occasional use on hair that is not especially thick, it is hard to argue with.

**Buy the InfinitiPro FloMotion Pro at about C$61** if you want the diffuser and the most capable Conair. But be aware that at about C$61 you are only **C$39 below the Laifen Air**, which sits in the price band where complaint rates fall from 30.3% to **9.6%**. That is a smaller gap than most buyers assume.

**Do not buy Conair if your hair is thick or long.** All three models are conventional heat-first designs, and our data shows airflow complaints run one way — reviewers never say a dryer is too powerful. See our [thick hair guide](/hair-types/thick-hair/) for what to buy instead.

**And check the ALCI plug and the cord strain relief on arrival**, whichever you choose. Cord and connector failure accounted for **417 mentions** in our dataset — see [how long hair dryers actually last](/guides/how-long-does-a-hair-dryer-last/).
